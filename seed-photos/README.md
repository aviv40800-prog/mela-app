# Demo-profile photos

Drop images (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`) into `female/` and `male/`. On the next server start they are copied into
the uploads store and assigned, in file-name order, to **demo** members of that gender who have no photos yet.
With N photos per member (e.g. 40 women's photos for 20 demo women), each gets up to 3.

Use only images you have the rights to use for a dating-app demo: your own shoot, models who signed a
release, or AI-generated faces. Most stock-photo licences forbid "sensitive use" such as dating profiles.

You can also add or replace any member's photos from the admin dashboard (`/admin/users/<id>`).
